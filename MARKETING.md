#### SECTION 6: NIGHT VISION FEATURE DISCLOSURE

**PLEASE READ THIS SECTION CAREFULLY. THE NIGHT VISION EXPERIENCE VARIES BASED ON YOUR CAMERA HARDWARE.**

Real Security Camera provides two distinct night vision modes. The mode available to you is determined entirely by the hardware capabilities of your camera, and is clearly labeled in the app at all times.

**6.1 — Hardware IR Night Vision (IP Cameras with Infrared Hardware)**

IP cameras equipped with physical infrared (IR) LED hardware — such as the ADC-V724 and similar models — support full remote-controlled hardware night vision. When this mode is available, the app provides a remote toggle to set IR mode to ON, OFF, or AUTO. Image quality in darkness is determined solely by the camera manufacturer's hardware specifications. Real Security Camera makes no warranty regarding the performance of third-party IR hardware.

**6.2 — Enhanced Night View (Software Enhancement for Phone Cameras)**

Smartphones and devices enrolled as cameras via the Real Security Camera app do not contain dedicated infrared hardware. For these devices, the app provides **Enhanced Night View**, a software-based image processing mode that applies brightness amplification, contrast enhancement, and grayscale filtering to improve low-light visibility.

**Enhanced Night View is a software image processing feature. It is NOT infrared night vision. It does NOT add hardware capabilities to your device. Results will vary based on the phone model, lens quality, ambient light conditions, and camera placement.**

The app clearly labels each camera's night vision type in the camera settings panel:
- **"Hardware IR Night Vision"** — for IP cameras with IR LEDs
- **"Enhanced Night View (Software)"** — for enrolled phone cameras

The Company makes no representation that Enhanced Night View will perform equivalently to hardware infrared night vision. Users with security requirements that depend on true zero-light visibility are advised to use dedicated IP cameras with hardware IR capability.

**The Company is NOT liable for any security incident, loss, or damage arising from a user's reliance on Enhanced Night View software processing in conditions requiring true infrared night vision hardware.**
